 How will I receive payment by Awin? 
 Awin offers a range of payment methods including SEPA, BACS, ACH, and Domestic payments in NOK, SEK, DKK, PLN, and CHF. For International Wire Transfers (IWT), we have partnered with Payoneer as our designated payment provider. This allows you to receive payments in a currency different from that of your bank country. To ensure a successful payment process, please complete the form within your publisher account.
