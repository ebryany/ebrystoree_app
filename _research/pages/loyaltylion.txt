 Frequently asked questions
 Why choose LoyaltyLion over other providers?
 +
 LoyaltyLion is trusted by small and enterprise brands for its flexibility, deep customization, and superior user experience for your customers.
 LoyaltyLion offers a fully embedded loyalty experience rather than relying on a pop-up widget, making it feel like a natural part of your store.
 Make your own rules and rewards, build flexible VIP tiers with exclusive perks, and integrate with 50+ tools to automate communications.
 Our platform segments customers into groups like Loyal, At Risk, and Win Back, allowing you to personalize communications.
 Ideal for omnichannel brands with multiple physical locations, LoyaltyLion lets your customers earn and redeem rewards online and in person.
 Plus, our top-rated support team guides you through every step of the journey!
 We’re proud to partner with you from day one to ensure you succeed at loyalty. See what our customers say on G2 !
 What ecommerce platforms do you support?
 +
 LoyaltyLion is made for Shopify and Shopify Plus .
 If your shop is built on Shopify you can create a custom loyalty program and connect to Shopify Point of Sale (POS) .
 If you use Shopify Plus, you can also offer gift card rewards, let customers redeem points at checkout and use the Shopify Flow connector to automate loyalty workflows.
 Does LoyaltyLion support omnichannel loyalty?
 +
 Yes! LoyaltyLion integrates with Shopify POS , allowing you to offer a seamless loyalty experience both online and in one or more physical stores. Customers can earn and redeem points across multiple locations.
 The Shopify integration is available for:
 Classic plan (Single location)
 Advanced plan (Multiple locations)
 Plus plan (Multiple locations)
 This ensures your loyalty program works seamlessly, no matter where your customers shop.
 Can I customize my loyalty program?
 +
 Absolutely! We work closely with you to build an on-brand loyalty program, starting with a name that resonates with your brand and audience.
 Your integrated Loyalty Page is designed and built by us, in collaboration with you. No development is required by your team. And, once you are up and running, our intuitive Theme Editor gives you total control of your integrated Loyalty Page’s look and feel as your brand and loyalty program evolves.
 Furthermore, with every program rule you set, your customers’ Loyalty Page automatically updates, making it easier than ever to optimize your program and run short promotions.
 Your loyalty page, referral links, receipt upload pages and loyalty pass install page can all sit on your own custom brand domain so every touchpoint feels like a natural part of your store.
