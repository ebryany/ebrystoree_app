 Frequently asked questions (FAQs) about Mekari Qontak
 What is Mekari Qontak?
 Mekari Qontak is an AI customer engagement platform integrated with an omnichannel CRM and an Official Meta Business Partner. As part of the unified Mekari ecosystem, it delivers a centralized, end-to-end customer data solution that enables businesses and enterprises to effectively boost sales, accelerate customer service, and optimize marketing and business operations through official WhatsApp API, omnichannel, CRM, AI chatbots, and campaign automation via a seamless SaaS platform.
 What is Mekari Qontak's main advantage over other platform?
 Mekari Qontak offers an integrated software solutions, not just separate features. All customer communications on WhatsApp, Instagram, e-commerce, and other channels can be managed from a single, centralized omnichannel dashboard. Equipped with CRM and automation features, the system is designed to boost your work efficiency.
 Who is Mekari Qontak suitable for?
 Mekari Qontak is suitable for use by small, medium and enterprise scale businesses in various industries such as UMKM/MSMEs, financial services, retail, startups, FMCG, logistics, automotive, education, health, multinationals, BUMN, and government.
 This solution is designed to help sales, customer service, marketing, and operational teams work more efficiently, responsively, and measurably to reach and serve customers.
 What solution plans does Mekari Qontak offer?
 Mekari Qontak offers four main solution plans tailored to your business needs:
 Broadcast : Send mass messages and promotions via WhatsApp.
 Service Suite : Enhance customer service with an integrated and responsive solution.
 Sales Suite : Manage sales and CRM efficiently.
 Qontak 360 : A comprehensive solution for the entire sales, marketing, and customer service team.
 Each plan is designed based on relevant needs to be measurable and easy to use in achieving your business goals.
 What are Mekari Qontak's key features?
 Mekari Qontak offers key features for your business such as the official WhatsApp API with a blue tick verification, a AI chatbot for automated conversations, and an omnichannel dashboard that integrates the management of WhatsApp, Instagram, Shopee, and Tokopedia. You can also use the CRM for sales pipeline management, a broadcast system for mass messages, and real-time reporting features to monitor overall performance.
 Is Mekari Qontak an official WhatsApp partner?
 Yes, Mekari Qontak is an official WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) registered and working directly with Meta. You can get an official WhatsApp Business account with a blue tick verification legally and reliably through our service.
 Can Mekari Qontak be integrated with other systems?
 Of course. Mekari Qontak provides flexible API integration that can be integrated with various internal systems, such as ERP, POS, e-commerce, or other platforms, according to your business needs.
 Can Mekari Qontak be customized to suit our business needs?
 Yes, Mekari Qontak is highly customizable to meet the specific needs of your business. You can tailor the system to fit your brand, configure workflows, create custom fields, and define unique processes that align with your business model. Our solution is designed to be flexible and scalable, capable of accommodating businesses of various sizes and industries.
 Is Mekari Qontak secure and trusted?
 Yes, Mekari Qontak is an official company that is safe, trusted, certified and legal. We take user data security and privacy very seriously. All company, consumer, and user data on Mekari Qontak is protected by robust security system measures.
 Mekari Qontak also complies with consumer privacy notice . We adhere to relevant data protection regulations, such as GDPR, to ensure your customer data is managed carefully and in line with legal requirements.
 Mekari Qontak is ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified . Additionally, it incorporates industry-standard security protocols, including data encryption, access controls, and regular backups.
 Is a free trial or demo period available for Mekari Qontak?
 Yes, free trials and demos and direct consultation session with our team are available so you can see how Mekari Qontak&#8217;s solution works for your specific business needs.
 How do I subscribe to Mekari Qontak?
 You can fill out the form on the Mekari Qontak website or contact our team directly for the activation process and to customize the solution for your company and business needs.
