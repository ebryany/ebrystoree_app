 FAQ about Mekari Qontak as a Customer Intelligence Solution (Omnichannel & CRM)
 What is Mekari Qontak and how does this solution help my business? 
 Mekari Qontak is a Customer Intelligence platform that integrates Omnichannel Communication and CRM systems to help businesses respond to customers faster, increase sales conversions, and deliver a more personal and efficient customer experience.
 How is Mekari Qontak's Omnichannel different from just multi-channel? 
 Omnichannel unifies all communication channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Email, Marketplace, etc.) into a single integrated dashboard. This allows agents to view a customer&#8217;s conversation history across channels without losing context—unlike multi-channel, where each platform operates independently.
 Can Mekari Qontak be used by both sales and customer service teams at the same time? 
 Yes. Mekari Qontak supports cross-functional use—from sales teams managing prospects and follow-ups, to customer service teams handling inquiries quickly and automatically via chatbots or live agents.
 What is the CRM like, and how flexible is it for my sales process? 
 Mekari Qontak’s CRM offers customizable pipelines, WhatsApp integration, GPS tracking for field sales, automated follow-ups, and performance dashboards that can be tailored to your business needs.
 Can I manage marketing campaigns from this platform too? 
 Absolutely. You can run segmented campaigns targeted at customers based on their interaction history and preferences. Campaigns can be sent via WhatsApp, SMS, email, and other channels—all integrated with the CRM for real-time tracking.
 How much does Mekari Qontak cost? 
 Mekari Qontak offers flexible pricing based on your business needs. Our solutions come in various packages such as Broadcast, Sales Suite, Service Suite, and Qontak 360, each designed to support different use cases. Pricing starts from IDR 750,000 per month and may vary depending on the selected package, feature coverage, and number of users.
 If you are unsure which package suits your business, our team is ready to help you find the right solution based on your customer engagement goals.
 What kind of after-sales support does Mekari Qontak provide? 
 Mekari Qontak is committed to supporting your business beyond onboarding. Here&#8217;s what you can expect:
 Implementation assistance for new clients , with support tailored based on the package selected. Each business receives the right level of guidance to ensure a smooth setup
 Support from our dedicated local team , who understands your business context
 Regular re-training sessions , designed to keep your team updated as your business grows or new features are introduced
 This support is designed to help businesses not only get started quickly but also stay productive and efficient in the long run.

 FAQ about Data Access and Security in Mekari Qontak
 Is my customer data safe with Mekari Qontak? 
 Yes, very safe. Mekari Qontak uses industry-standard encryption and trusted cloud infrastructure. All data sent or stored is protected with multiple layers of security.
 Can I control who has access to certain data? 
 Yes. Mekari Qontak offers role-based access control, allowing you to restrict who can view, edit, or manage data based on their department or role within the organization.
 Where is my data stored? 
 Your data is stored in cloud servers located in regions that comply with data protection regulations and maintain high SLAs for uptime and service availability.
 How does audit trail or activity tracking work? 
 Mekari Qontak logs all user activities through an integrated tracking system. This allows you to audit and review every action taken within the platform transparently.
 Is Mekari Qontak compliant with Indonesia’s data protection regulations? 
 Yes. Mekari Qontak is designed to comply with Indonesia’s personal data protection laws (including the PDP Law) and is continuously updated in line with regulatory changes.
