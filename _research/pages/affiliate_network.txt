 What is affiliate marketing?
 Affiliate marketing is a form of performance marketing, generated by referrals from a partner site to your site. The partner is awarded a commission based on every completed purchase they drive for you (the advertiser). Read more about what affiliate marketing is and how affiliate marketing can help your business .
 Is affiliate marketing right for my business? 
 Affiliate marketing is a cost-efficient way for online businesses of any size to reach new customers and grow their own way through valuable online partnerships.As long as you've got an ecommerce store or website, affiliate marketing is a performance driven way to spend your marketing budget.
By using a market-leading affiliate platform like Awin you'll be able to securely; search and discover partners, report on results, and reward partners. Online affiliate partnerships will open your business up to a vast number of new customers that you won't have access to through other online channels.
 What does it cost me to work with Awin?
 Awin Access is our self-managed platform offer that allows you to join for free for the first month. Starting from the second month, you'll benefit from an affordable monthly platform fee of just $49.
 We offer two additional options: Awin Accelerate and Awin Advanced . With these more comprehensive platform offers, you have the flexibility to self-manage your programme or leverage expert account management and advice.
 Can I still join Awin if I don't have an ecommerce store?
 To qualify for our Awin affiliate program, you'll need to have an ecommerce website where you sell either a product or a service. This requirement ensures our affiliate partners can promote your business and drive traffic to your site, resulting in successful and trackable sales.
 Can I work with partners in different countries?
 Awin operates in several markets across the UK, Europe, the US, Australia and Brazil. You also have access to other Asia-Pacific countries through Commission Factory .
By joining our platform, you gain access to a global network of partners. However, if you wish to work with multiple partners overseas, you'll need to complete an application form and establish a program separately for each region. This means that you'll have a fully localized program in each market, which gives you advantages:
• Not all partners have global bank accounts, so some may be hesitant to participate in a program outside of their currency, potentially losing out on foreign exchange rates.
• Some advertisers prefer to work strategically with partners in specific markets, rather than others, so you can split out the program accordingly.
• Finally, having a fully localized program in the local language will always result in greater engagement from partners in that market.
 Can I change my plan?
 Upgrading your platform offer with Awin is a straightforward process. To do so, simply fill out your details on our website form , and our team will guide you through the process.
If you're on our Awin Accelerate or Awin Advanced platform offers and want to downgrade, you will need to wait until the end of your current contract to make any changes.
 How do I integrate my website with Awin's platform? 
 We provide options for you to integrate your website with our platform, based on the complexity of your needs or the support required.
Our easy-to-follow self-managed wizard will guide you through program integration step-by-step, or you can select one of our Technical Integration Add-ons if you need more support. Plugins for popular ecommerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce and Magento are also available.
 What service do I get with Awin?
 Awin Access is our self-managed affiliate plan, giving you full control. While we don’t offer account management for this plan, we provide free training webinars, an Advertiser Success Center, and support via email. Add tailored service add-ons for extra support. Alternatively, with Awin Accelerate and Awin Advanced get all of this, plus personalized account management. Whether you prefer more hands-on assistance or minimal intervention, we are here to provide the level of support that best fit your needs. Explore Awin's plans now.
