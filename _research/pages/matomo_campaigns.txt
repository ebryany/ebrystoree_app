 Matomo helps you understand how visitors arrive at your website by tracking their acquisition sources and marketing channels . This includes identifying whether a visit came from a search engine, a social media platform, a paid campaign, a link on another website, or by typing your URL directly into the browser.
 Understanding acquisition is essential for evaluating the effectiveness of your marketing efforts and optimising your channels. Matomo groups visits by Channel Type to give you a high-level view of traffic sources, and uses Referrer information to attribute visits and conversions to specific campaigns or sites.
