 Affiliate Marketing & Software FAQs
 This FAQ is designed for owners and marketers. We’ll directly address your most pressing questions about starting and scaling an affiliate program without the headache.
 How do I know if I’ll actually make money with an affiliate program?
 Affiliate marketing is a performance-based model, which makes it one of the most cost-effective marketing channels. You only pay commissions after a successful sale has been made. Tapfiliate helps you maximize your ROI by giving you the tools to track every single click and conversion, so you can see exactly how much revenue your affiliates are generating against their commissions.
 What is your pricing model? Are there any hidden fees?
 We believe in transparency. Tapfiliate offers simple monthly plans based on your program’s usage (like clicks and conversions). Crucially, we do not charge a transaction fee on your sales. The revenue you earn is yours. There are no surprise costs, allowing for predictable monthly expenses as you grow.
 Will Tapfiliate work with my storefront / stack without developer help?
 Yes. Pre‑built connectors for Shopify, WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix, BigCommerce, Stripe, Paddle and 30+ others install in <10 min. If you’re on a custom site, drop in the JS snippet or call the REST API.
 Can I make the affiliate program look like my own brand?
 Yes. A seamless brand experience builds trust with both customers and affiliates. Tapfiliate is fully white-label, meaning you can customize the affiliate dashboard, sign-up pages, and links with your own logo, colors, and even host it on your own custom domain. It will feel like an extension of your own website, not a third-party tool.
