 What is deep linking?
 Deep linking takes users directly to specific in-app content, like a product page, a promo, or a personalized experience, instead of just launching the app’s homepage.
 What is deferred deep linking?
 Say a user taps a link in your ad: Deferred deep linking lets you send users to the right in-app content even if they don’t have your app yet.
 Here’s how it works:
 If they have the app, the link will route them to the desired in-app content.
 If they don’t have the app, the link will route them to install the app and then — with context still in place — to the desired in-app content.
