 Frequently asked questions
 What exactly is UGC and how does it differ from visual UGC? 
 UGC, or user-generated content , is the umbrella term for content that comes from your customers. This includes: reviews, star ratings, photos, and videos. Visual UGC, in particular, refers to photos and videos that are either collected via reviews or curated from social media.
 Why is UGC so powerful for eCommerce brands? 
 UGC posted on a brand’s site or social media can inspire shoppers to make a purchase. UGC expedites the customer journey by boosting buyer confidence, reinforcing brand recognition, and meeting shoppers where they are on mobile. Learn more about the power of UGC and Visual UGC here .
 Can you use UGC without permission? 
 We recommend requesting permission from the user if you’d like to repost their photo or image to your Instagram. However, if you’ve sent one of your customers a review request, you don’t need permission to publish their review, star rating, or any images or photos they may include. Learn more about permissions here .
 How do UGC creators make money? 
 UGC creators usually make money through sponsored posts and affiliated marketing, but there are numerous ways for creators to work with brands and get paid to do so.
