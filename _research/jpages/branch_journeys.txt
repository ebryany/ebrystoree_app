Smart banners

Web-to-app banners that actually convert

Display banners on your website drive users exactly where you want them: a product page, signup flow, or specific app content.

*   User viewing a product on web? Send them to the exact product page in your app.
*   Reading an article? Same article, now in your app.
*   Browsing a category? Deep link directly to that category in your app.

![Image 15: Illustration demonstrating the creation, preview, and A/B testing of banner ads](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/smart-banners-1.svg)

Deep linking

No app, no problem

When a user doesn’t have your app installed, one tap takes them to the App Store or Google Play. After install, [deferred deep linking](https://www.branch.io/glossary/deferred-deep-linking/) avoids dead ends by preserving context and delivering them to the original content they expected.

![Image 16](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/deep-linking-1-scaled.webp)

Targeting

Precision targeting, not blasts

Show different messages based on device, location, behavior, purchase history, app install status, and referring source. This isn’t just audience building — it’s delivering the right message to the right user at the right moment.

*   Target users who don’t have the app with install prompts
*   Welcome back lapsed users with personalized CTAs
*   Exclude users who already converted
*   Treat Google search traffic differently to protect SEO efforts

![Image 17](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/targeting.webp)

No dev required

Build and launch without engineering

Use visual editing and more than 20 templates to launch when you’re ready. You own the creative, targeting, and schedule — no dev queue required.

![Image 18](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/no-dev-required-1440x929.webp)

A/B testing

Test everything, scale what wins

Run [A/B variations](https://www.branch.io/resources/blog/maximizing-conversions-tips-for-a-b-testing-branch-journeys/) on creative, copy, and CTAs. See conversion rates in real time and instantly shift traffic to winners.

![Image 19](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/a-b-testing.webp)

Measurement

Full-funnel visibility

From views to conversions, see the complete picture. Track every twist, turn, and branch-out in your funnel. Slice by campaign, device, OS, or any dimension that matters. Know exactly where Banners drove value and where revenue actually came from.

![Image 20](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/branch-banners-measurement.png)

Attribution

Your paid channels keep credit

If a user comes from a paid ad and converts through Banners, attribution goes to the original ad. No channel cannibalization, so your measurement stays clean.

## **Branch Banners frequently asked questions (FAQs)**

****What is Branch Banners?****
Branch Banners is an enterprise-grade [web-to-app](https://help.branch.io/marketer-hub/docs/web-to-app)conversion platform. It enables brands to deploy [smart banners](https://www.branch.io/glossary/smart-banner/) and interstitials on mobile and desktop web to route users seamlessly into their native apps.

**What happens if a user doesn’t have my app installed?**
Branch uses [deferred deep linking](https://www.branch.io/glossary/deferred-deep-linking/) to preserve context through install. The user is sent to the appropriate app store to download the app, and upon opening it for the first time, automatically taken to the specific content they were viewing on the web.

**Can I target specific banners to different web audiences?**
Yes. You can segment audiences based on behavior, purchase history, and install status to show the most relevant banner to every visitor.

**Do I need a developer to update banner creative?**
No. Branch Banners provides a visual editor and over 20 templates, so you can launch and edit without engineering help.

**How does Branch Banners handle iOS Private Relay and ATT?**
Branch is built for a privacy-first world, helping you reclaim attribution and maintain accurate web-to-app measurement while staying compliant with Apple’s latest frameworks.
