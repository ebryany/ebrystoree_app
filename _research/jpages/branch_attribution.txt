Make better investment decisions with unified attribution for paid, owned, and earned channels in one centralized dashboard. Branch provides the infrastructure to capture the entire omnichannel shopping journey across every touchpoint.

[Attribution](https://www.branch.io/glossary/attribution/) connects your marketing efforts to real outcomes. See which campaigns drive conversions, understand the complete customer journey, and measure impact where it matters — not just where it’s easy.

![Image 19: Green dollar sign symbol representing ROI](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/ROI-icon-green.svg)
### Invest with confidence

When you see true performance across every channel, you know exactly where your budget should go. Spot top performers, cut what’s not working, and allocate resources to your highest-ROI opportunities.

![Image 20: Green dashboard icon](https://www.branch.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/dashboard-icon-green.svg)
### See one source of truth for every channel

One dashboard means one truth. Unified attribution lets you stack every channel side-by-side so you can finally compare experimental campaigns against evergreen ones and see exactly what drives results. No platform bias, just clear answers.

## Two steps to better attribution

**1. Start tracking all user touchpoints**

User journeys happen across channels and devices. To get accurate attribution, you need to measure every interaction. Branch has a years-long track record of powering flawless cross-channel user experiences on both digital and offline channels.

**2. Capture the entire cross-platform journey**

Your customer journeys aren’t linear, so your attribution shouldn’t be either. Influence and engagement happen on any platform, and to understand the full return on your marketing investments, you need to track all of your campaigns. Branch attribution provides a deduplicated, unbiased view across paid, owned, and earned channels. With last-touch attribution by default, our attribution reporting is free from systemic bias toward any specific channel or source — designed instead to give useful insights for omnichannel marketing strategies. But you don’t see only the last touch. You see the full journey. Branch Funnel Analysis lets you track every interaction that led to conversion so you can make the most informed decisions about where to invest.

## Branch attribution frequently asked questions (FAQs)

**What is attribution?**
Attribution shows you what actually drives results by capturing every interaction between users and your brand and connecting conversions to the interactions that drove them. Without unified measurement, every channel looks like a winner. Unified attribution shows you which ones really are.

**Why is omnichannel attribution important?**
Customer journeys don’t happen in one channel. People move across devices, platforms, and touchpoints before they convert. Omnichannel attribution eliminates data silos by providing a complete view of how ads, web, email, and social interactions work together to drive app engagement.  
  
Branch brings paid, owned, and earned into one unified view — cross-platform, cross-device, cross-channel. You see the complete journey so you can make smarter investment decisions.

**How does Branch support privacy-safe measurement?**
Branch uses a compliance-first infrastructure to provide accurate attribution while adhering to regulations like GDPR and CCPA. We turn privacy into a competitive advantage with multi-touch attribution models that help you grow while respecting user data through advanced multi-touch attribution models.
