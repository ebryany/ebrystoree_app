Title: Discounts

URL Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts

Markdown Content:
Offering discounts can be a marketing strategy that you use to drive sales in your Shopify store. To offer discounts, you can create discount codes or set up automatic discounts, or you can alternatively [set sale prices for individual products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-pricing/sale-pricing).

You can create codes for a monetary value discount, a percentage discount, a buy X get Y discount, or a free shipping discount. Customers can enter discount codes online at checkout, or in person if you're using [Shopify POS](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/discount-management).

You can also create other types of discount by using a [third-party app from the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/categories/marketing-and-conversion-promotions-discounts/all). Some third-party apps let you manage discounts directly in your Shopify admin, or you might need to create your discounts in the app's settings.

If your store is on the Shopify Plus plan, then you can [create your own custom discounts app](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/apps/about-apps#custom-apps).

When you offer a discount on an order, the discount is applied to the subtotal of the order before taxes, and then any applicable tax is applied afterwards.

You can update and manage existing discounts from the **Discounts** page in your Shopify admin. Before you update a discount, check if another staff member might also be making changes to the same discount.

You can also use a [rollout](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/rollouts) to schedule when a discount is activated or deactivated, or to test a discount against the discounts that your store offers already. Learn more about [discounts in a rollout](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/managing-discounts#rollouts).

You can also ask [Sidekick](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/ai-powered-tools/sidekick), Shopify's built-in, free AI commerce assistant, to help create, edit, or troubleshoot discounts in your Shopify admin. For example, ask: "Create a 15% discount code for my summer collection, one use per customer, ending Sunday."
